## 1. Purpose & status

- This register records **candidate legal/regulatory obligations and uncertainties** for epicRider, a platform targeting the Indonesian market (multimedia/event/production/broadcast/rental operations, personal data via user profiles and hiRider candidate profiles, digital commerce via license/extension stores, IMS/compliance evidence, and developer commerce).
- It contains **NO legal conclusions**. Every entry is a candidate obligation or an uncertainty until validated by a competent legal professional. OD-09 ("Kewajiban hukum/privasi lokal (UU PDP dll.) — butuh ahli") is **OPEN**; no counsel has been appointed and no validation has occurred in this phase.
- Per the reconciled baseline, OD-09:
  - does **not** block generic architecture (domain model, logical data model, threat model, authorization, entitlement, API boundaries);
  - **does** block legal applicability claims, compliance sign-off, and certification/legal assertions until validated by competent parties.
- This register is the engineering-side uncertainty record required by that rule (`SECURITY.md` → Legal/regulatory: "Engineering must record candidate obligations and evidence without declaring legal applicability"; Phase 2 prompt §5 OD-09: record as an uncertainty register, make no legal conclusion).
- **This document is not legal advice and may not be cited as a determination that any instrument applies or does not apply to epicRider.**

## 2. Scope & method

**In scope:** candidate obligations surfaced from the product's own features and documents — personal data processing, digital commerce, electronic signatures, copyright of standards text, audit/evidence records, notifications, workforce marketplace (kru), sensitive metadata capture, cross-border hosting aspects, accessibility, minors (checked), sustainability claims.

**Sources = project documents only.** Entries were derived by reading: `MASTER_CONTROL.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, `LICENSE_MATRIX.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `TESTING.md`, `CONTROL_CONSISTENCY_MATRIX.md`, `PHASE_2_DEPENDENCY_MATRIX.md`, the Phase 2 prompt (§5, §16), `REQUIREMENTS.md` (REQ-001..REQ-100), `OPEN_DECISIONS.md`, `DOCUMENT_EVIDENCE_MODEL.md`, `NON_FUNCTIONAL_REQUIREMENTS.md`, `RISK_REGISTER.md`, `PRODUCT_SCOPE.md`, `USE_CASES.md`, `PERSONAS_AND_JOURNEYS.md`, `UX_REQUIREMENTS.md`, `STANDARD_REGISTER.md`.

**NO external legal research was performed or verified in this phase.** No statute text, official gazette, regulator guidance, or legal database was consulted. Instrument names below are **CANDIDATES, NOT VERIFIED**:
- "UU PDP" is named as a candidate in project documents (`OPEN_DECISIONS.md` OD-09, `RISK_REGISTER.md` RSK-113, `PRODUCT_SCOPE.md` X-8); a candidate number ("UU No. 27/2022") is attached from general knowledge only.

**Method:** feature → affected data/record/flow → candidate instrument (NOT VERIFIED) → generic architectural implication (data model / authorization / retention / export / logging — no legal conclusion) → validation question (what, by whom) → related existing OD. LR-001..LR-018 is a new namespace owned by this register; all REQ-/OD-/RSK-/UXR-/NFR-/X- references are existing IDs verified against the source documents. No OD or REQ IDs were invented.

**Status vocabulary** follows `MASTER_CONTROL.md` §3 / `TESTING.md`: `VERIFIED / PARTIAL / NOT FOUND / NOT TESTED / BLOCKED / PROPOSED / UNKNOWN — NEEDS VALIDATION` (decision side: `OPEN / PROPOSED / ACCEPTED / REJECTED / SUPERSEDED`). ## 5. What this register does NOT do

- **No applicability determinations** — nothing here decides whether any instrument applies to epicRider, any feature, or any data flow.
- **No compliance sign-offs** — OD-09 explicitly blocks compliance sign-off until validated by competent parties; this register provides zero sign-off.
- **No legal opinions** — entries are candidate obligations and uncertainties, phrased as "may implicate", "candidate", "needs validation".
- **No vendor/regulator commitments** — no provider, authority, or certification body is named as engaged, consulted, or committed to.
- **No claims that any law does or does not apply** — including silence on a topic (e.g., LR-016, LR-017) which is recorded as UNKNOWN/NOT FOUND, never as "not applicable".
- **No instrument is asserted to be current, correctly numbered, or in force** — all instrument names are candidates marked NOT VERIFIED.
- **Engineering cannot close LR entries.** Validation owner: **owner + competent legal counsel (OD-09)**, with per-entry support: tax advisor (LR-007), employment-law-competent counsel (LR-011), IMS lead (LR-009, LR-013, LR-018), hosting decision participants (LR-012: OD-10/OD-114).
